College History

The University of Technology and Applied Sciences (UTAS) in Oman was established to support technical education and applied learning. It aims to prepare students with practical skills aligned with market needs.

Mission

To provide high-quality applied education that develops skilled graduates capable of contributing to Oman’s development.

Vision

To be a leading institution in applied sciences and technology education in the region.

Facilities

  • Modern computer labs
  • Engineering workshops
  • Library with digital resources
  • Smart classrooms
  • Student support center
  • Wi-Fi enabled campus
